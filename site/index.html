Research question

What survives the neural observation process?

The simulator gives us ground truth. Circular and linear task trajectories are mapped into heterogeneous neural populations, converted to stochastic spike counts, and encoded with PCA or CNN1D. Recovery is measured against the latent process that generated them.

Initial benchmark

Recovery becomes harder as latent structure is enriched

0.740Circular 3D · PCA
0.545Circular 3D · CNN1D
0.807Linear 2D · PCA
0.794Linear 2D · CNN1D

The 5D circular and 4D linear variants retain moderate distance geometry, but coordinate recovery degrades. This is an experimental result, not evidence that a larger latent is intrinsically worse: loading strength, objective design, and validation remain ablations.

Generative and learning blocks

Known latent → neural drive → spikes → representation

  1. 01Task latent

    Generate circular or linear trajectories with interpretable coordinates.

  2. 02Population map

    Assign direction, position, mixed, contextual, or localized place tuning.

  3. 03Spike emission

    Map through neural drive and a positive rate to stochastic binned counts.

  4. 04Representation

    Fit PCA and a temporal CNN to identical centered neural windows.

  5. 05Evaluation

    Compare embeddings with ground truth using Procrustes R² and RSA.
